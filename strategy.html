How we work

No big bang. A staged path that earns its confidence.

Most AI projects fail because they start with the tool, not the workflow. We start where the time and the risk actually are, prove value on a small surface, then scale what works.

01
0 to 1 month

Assess

Map the workflows, the data and the quick wins. Where is the time really going, what is safe to automate first, and what must stay human-led? You get a clear, costed picture before anyone commits to software.

02
1 to 3 months

Pilot

Run controlled pilots on a few high-volume tasks with proper guardrails. We measure time saved and quality honestly, so the decision to scale rests on evidence rather than enthusiasm.

03
3 to 9 months

Scale

Roll out what works, train the team, and set the governance and standards. Freed capacity gets redeployed into advisory and higher-margin work, not cut from the payroll.

04
Ongoing

Lead

Make AI fluency part of how the firm operates, and a reason clients choose you. Fractional AI leadership keeps standards current as the tools and the rules keep moving.
