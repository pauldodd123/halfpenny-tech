About the group

A holding company for work that has to actually work.

Halfpenny Technologies Limited is the parent company behind the Halfpenny brands. It exists to keep each company small, focused and independent, while sharing the things worth sharing: standards, design, and two decades of hard-won experience.

The thread across everything is the same: build things that are honest, owned by the people who use them, and good enough to stand behind. No lock-in, no bloat, no theatre.

The group is founder-led and UK-based, working with professional firms, founders and technical teams who want substance over noise.
